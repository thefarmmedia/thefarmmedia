You're On A Job

Phone tag kills momentum. Homeowners book their own on-site estimate straight from your website — pick a day, pick a time, done — without you or your team stopping mid-job to answer the phone.

What's Included

How Booking Works

  • Live calendar embedded directly on your website
  • Automatic sync with your real schedule — no double-bookings
  • Text and email confirmation the moment someone books
  • Automated reminders leading up to the appointment to cut down no-shows
  • Easy rescheduling links so a change doesn't mean a phone call
  • Every booking lands in your CRM as a tracked lead

Every round of back-and-forth texting to land on a date and time is a chance for a homeowner to lose momentum and go book with whoever responded easiest. Self-service booking removes that friction entirely — the moment they're ready, they can lock in a time, and your calendar fills up without you or your team stopping mid-job to coordinate it.

Questions

Online Booking FAQ

Does this sync with my existing calendar?

Yes — it syncs with your real schedule so you never get double-booked, and blocks off time you've already marked unavailable.

Can customers reschedule themselves?

Yes. Every confirmation includes a link to reschedule or cancel without needing to call your office.

What if I want to talk to the homeowner before booking an estimate?

We can set the flow up either way — instant self-booking, or a request that your team confirms first. Most clients start with instant booking and adjust based on what fits their process.

Does this replace talking to customers on the phone?

No — it just removes the scheduling back-and-forth. You can still call to confirm details or answer questions; the booking itself just doesn't require it.

What if I need to block off time for an existing job that runs long?

You can block time manually any time, and the calendar respects whatever's already on it — no double-bookings.

Can I require a deposit to book an estimate?

That can be built in if it fits how you operate, though most clients keep the estimate booking itself free and simple to maximize how many people follow through.
