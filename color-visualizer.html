Color Visualizer

Let Homeowners See Their
Color Before They Call

Picking a flake or metallic color from a tiny sample chip is hard. The color visualizer tool lets a homeowner select a color on your site and instantly get emailed real photos of that exact color installed in a local garage — so they know what they're getting before they ever pick up the phone.

How It Works

From Color Chip To Confident Buyer

  • Homeowner browses your real color options on your website
  • They select the color they're considering for their space
  • The tool automatically emails them real installed photos of that color
  • Their email is captured as a lead the moment they request photos
  • Built for Ozarks Concrete Coatings and adaptable to your own color line
  • Works on mobile, where most homeowners are actually browsing

Color is one of the hardest decisions in this entire process for a homeowner, and it's usually made from a tiny sample chip under bad lighting. That uncertainty is exactly what stalls a decision — nobody wants to commit thousands of dollars to a color they're not sure about. Real installed photos remove that uncertainty in a way no sample chip ever could.

Questions

Color Visualizer FAQ

Do I need my own project photos for this to work?

Yes — the tool works best when it's sending real photos of your own completed jobs in each color. If you're just getting started, we can help plan out what photos to prioritize capturing first.

Can I use this for more than one product line?

Yes. It can be set up for flake, metallic, or solid color systems — whatever color options you actually offer.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes — it's built mobile-first, since most homeowners are browsing colors on their phone.

How many colors can the tool support?

As many as you actually offer. It's built around your real product line, not a generic set of options.

What if I don't have installed photos in every color yet?

We help prioritize which colors to photograph first based on what you sell most, and can expand the tool's photo library over time as you complete more jobs.

Does this replace an in-person consultation?

No — it's a step before that, not a replacement. It gets a homeowner confident enough in a color to book the consultation in the first place.
