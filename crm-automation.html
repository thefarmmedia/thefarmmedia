Questions

CRM & Automation FAQ

What CRM platform do you use?

We build on a GoHighLevel-based CRM subaccount, set up and configured specifically for your business — no generic out-of-the-box template.

Can I keep my existing CRM?

In most cases we recommend consolidating into the system we build, since it's directly wired into your website and calculator. If you have a reason to keep a separate system, we can talk through what integration looks like.

Does this replace my office staff?

No — it's built to support whoever answers your phones, not replace them. It makes sure no lead waits hours (or days) for a response, even when your team is slammed.

Do I need to be tech-savvy to use this?

No. The interface is built to be simple — see your leads, see what stage they're in, see what's been automated. We handle the technical setup.

What happens if a lead doesn't respond to automated follow-up?

The system keeps a lead active in the pipeline and can trigger reactivation campaigns weeks or months later — a lead that goes quiet isn't necessarily a lost lead.

Can multiple team members use the CRM?

Yes. Everyone who handles leads can have their own login and visibility into the pipeline, so nothing depends on one person checking one inbox.
