The Concrete Jobs System: A Lead Flow
Framework For Coating Contractors

Updated August 2026 5 min read The Farm Media

Most concrete coating companies don't have a marketing problem. They have a system problem — leads come in, but nothing consistent happens after that. The Concrete Jobs System is the framework we build into every client site to fix exactly that.

The Five Steps

1. Homeowner Searches

They find you through Google, Maps, or Facebook while comparing contractors — usually multiple tabs open, multiple companies in consideration at once.

2. Instant Price

Instead of a generic "call for a quote," they get a real price range in under a minute from your branded calculator. This is the step that separates you from every competitor still hiding their pricing.

3. Lead Capture

To get the full quote, they submit their contact info — landing directly in a tracked CRM pipeline, not a shared inbox someone checks at the end of the day.

4. We Follow Up

Automated texts and emails keep the lead warm immediately, with a human follow-up layered on top. Speed here is the single biggest lever on close rate — most homeowners go with whoever responds first.

5. Booked Job

More qualified conversations turn into more estimates on the calendar, and more estimates turn into more jobs won.

We don't just drive leads. We build systems that book jobs — the difference is what happens in the hours after someone fills out a form.

Why Most Companies Are Missing Steps 2 Through 4

Plenty of concrete coating websites nail step 1 — they show up in search, or run some ads, and get traffic. Very few have steps 2 through 4 actually built. That's the gap: traffic without a system to catch and convert it is just an expensive way to generate impressions.

What It Looks Like In Practice

On a real client's 30-day campaign, this system produced 115 messaging conversations and 40 form leads from a modest ad budget — every one of them tracked down to cost-per-conversation, not just vague reach numbers. That's the system working end to end, not just one piece of it.
