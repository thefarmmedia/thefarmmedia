Facebook Ads vs. Google Ads For
Concrete Coating Companies

Updated August 2026 4 min read The Farm Media

Both platforms work for concrete coating companies, but they work differently — and running the wrong one first is a common way to burn budget without much to show for it.

Facebook Ads: Best For Getting Started

Facebook and Instagram ads reach people who aren't actively searching yet — you're putting your work in front of homeowners scrolling their feed, using photo and video of real jobs to create demand rather than waiting for it. For most concrete coating companies just getting started, this tends to produce a lower cost per lead, since you're not competing directly against every other contractor bidding on the same search term.

It's also the better fit for showcasing the kind of visual, satisfying transformation concrete coating naturally has — before-and-afters, flake broadcast in action, a finished floor catching the light. That kind of content performs well as an ad and costs far less to produce than most people assume.

Google Ads: Best For Capturing Ready-To-Buy Searches

Google Ads puts you in front of homeowners actively typing "epoxy garage floor cost" or "concrete coating near me" — high intent, but also higher cost per click, since you're bidding directly against every competitor for that same keyword. This works best once you already have a system in place to convert that traffic, since you're paying a premium for people who are close to deciding.

Facebook creates demand. Google captures demand that already exists. Most companies need to build the first before the second is worth the spend.

Our Usual Recommendation

Start with Facebook to build a predictable, lower-cost lead flow and prove out your calculator funnel and follow-up system. Once that's dialed in and converting well, layer in Google Ads to capture the higher-intent searchers actively comparing contractors right now. Running both from day one usually just splits a limited budget too thin to learn much from either.

What Actually Determines Success

Neither platform fixes a broken funnel. The ad is only as good as what it points to — a fast, mobile-first site with a real price on it and a CRM that follows up within minutes, not hours. Get that right first, and either platform (or eventually both) will perform far better than they would pointed at a generic "call us" page.
