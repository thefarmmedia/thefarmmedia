5 Signs Your Concrete Coating Website
Isn't Converting

Updated August 2026 4 min read The Farm Media

You can be booked out on great reviews and word of mouth and still have a website that's quietly costing you jobs. Here's how to tell if yours is one of them.

1. There's No Price Anywhere On The Site

"Call for a free estimate" is the default on almost every concrete coating website — and it's exactly why homeowners bounce to the next tab. People comparing five contractors want a ballpark before they commit to a phone call. If your site doesn't give them one, a competitor's instant price calculator will.

2. It Takes More Than One Tap To Contact You On Mobile

Most of your traffic is on a phone, standing in a driveway or scrolling on the couch. If your phone number isn't a single tap-to-call button visible without scrolling, you're losing people to friction alone.

3. The Photos Don't Look Like Your Actual Work

Stock photos of generic garages, or five-year-old phone pictures with bad lighting, undersell craftsmanship that deserves better. Homeowners are trying to picture their own garage — real, well-lit photos of real jobs do more selling than any paragraph of copy.

4. There's No System Behind The Contact Form

A contact form that emails a shared inbox nobody checks until the end of the day is a leak, not a lead system. If a submission doesn't trigger an instant notification and land in a tracked pipeline, you're relying on someone remembering to check email — every hour that passes drops your odds of a callback converting.

Speed is the most underrated advantage in this industry. The company that responds first usually wins the job, regardless of price.

5. It Isn't Built To Rank

A beautiful site that nobody finds doesn't book jobs. If your site was built without local SEO in mind — proper page structure, a real Google Business Profile connection, location-specific content — you're relying entirely on paid ads or word of mouth to bring anyone to it at all.

What To Do About It

None of these are hard fixes individually, but they add up. A site that's missing all five is worth rebuilding from scratch rather than patching — which is exactly the kind of project we build for concrete coating companies specifically, with the calculator, the CRM, and the SEO foundation built in from day one.
