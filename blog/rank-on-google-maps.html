How To Rank On Google Maps For
“Epoxy Garage Floor Near Me”

Updated August 2026 5 min read The Farm Media

When someone searches "epoxy garage floor near me," Google shows a map with three businesses above every organic result. Those three spots — the "map pack" — get the overwhelming majority of clicks for local searches like that one. Here's what actually moves you into it.

1. Claim And Fully Build Out Your Google Business Profile

This is non-negotiable and it's free. Complete every field: categories, service area, hours, photos, and a real business description. An incomplete profile is one of the fastest ways to lose to a competitor who filled theirs out properly.

2. Get Reviews — And Get Them Consistently

Review count and rating are a real ranking factor, not just a trust signal. A steady stream of new reviews outperforms a big pile of old ones. This is exactly why automated review requests sent right after a job wraps matter more than most contractors realize — timing and consistency beat a one-time push.

3. Match Your Website To Your Service Area

If your Google Business Profile says you serve five counties but your website never mentions any of them by name, you're leaving relevance signals on the table. Real, specific content about the cities and areas you actually serve helps Google understand where to show you.

4. Get Cited Consistently Across The Web

Your business name, address, and phone number should match exactly across every directory, listing, and citation — Yelp, Angi, Facebook, industry-specific directories. Inconsistent information (a missing suite number, an old phone number) quietly undermines the trust signals Google uses to rank local businesses.

5. Build A Site That's Actually Fast And Mobile-First

Page speed and mobile usability are baked into how Google evaluates local search results. A slow, desktop-first site with a bloated page builder is fighting an uphill battle before a single word of content matters.

Most concrete coating companies haven't touched their local SEO at all. In a market like that, doing the fundamentals well is often enough to outrank the competition.

How Long It Takes

Most businesses start seeing real movement in local rankings within 60 to 90 days of consistent work, with bigger gains compounding over six months.
