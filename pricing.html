Pricing Questions

Common Questions

How does pricing work — one-time or monthly?

Both. Your website, branded to your business with an instant price calculator built in, is a one-time build starting at $2,499. Once it's live, you choose an ongoing monthly growth package — Foundation or Scale — to run ads, SEO, and automation on top of it.

Is there a contract or can I cancel anytime?

No long-term contract is required on either monthly package. We ask for a 90-day minimum so the systems have time to actually generate data and results, then it's month to month.

Is ad spend included in the monthly price?

No — the monthly price covers our management, systems, and build work. Ad spend is paid directly to Facebook/Google and is separate, with suggested ranges listed per package above.

Can I upgrade from Foundation to Scale later?

Yes. Most clients start on Foundation while running a single crew, then move up to Scale once they're ready to run a second crew and want active ad management, retargeting, and full automation.

What's not included in the website build or packages?

Ad spend itself, third-party software fees beyond our CRM platform, and one-off branding work like logo design are billed separately or scoped as needed.
