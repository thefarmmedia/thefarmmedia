Questions

Website Design FAQ

How long does a new website take to launch?

Most builds are live within 2 to 3 weeks of kickoff, including your branded instant price calculator and CRM setup.

Do you use website templates?

No. Every site is custom-designed around your brand, your photos, and your service area — not a recycled theme with your logo swapped in.

What if I already have a website?

We can rebuild it from scratch or migrate your existing content and branding into a new, faster, lead-focused site. Either way, we start with a strategy call to figure out what's actually working and what isn't.

What platform do you build websites on?

We hand-build on lightweight, custom HTML and CSS rather than a bloated page builder like Wix or WordPress loaded with plugins. That means faster load times, fewer security headaches, and no monthly builder fees quietly eating into your margin.

Can I update the website myself after it launches?

Yes. We set you up to make basic text and photo updates yourself, and we're always available for anything bigger — new pages, seasonal promotions, service area changes.

Do you write the copy too, or do I need to provide it?

Copywriting is included. We write every page around how homeowners in your specific market actually search and what gets them to pick up the phone — not generic stock copy pulled from a template.
