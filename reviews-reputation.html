Questions

Reviews & Reputation FAQ

What happens if I get a bad review?

We help you respond professionally and, where appropriate, take the conversation offline to resolve it. A single bad review with a thoughtful response often builds more trust than no bad reviews at all.

How do you request reviews?

Automated text messages go out at the right moment after a job — typically once the coating has cured and the customer has seen the finished result.

Does this work with my existing Google Business Profile?

Yes — we connect directly to your existing listing rather than starting over. If you don't have one set up yet, we'll set it up as part of the process.
