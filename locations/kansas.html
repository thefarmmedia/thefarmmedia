A Growing Suburban Market

Built For Your Market

New construction means new garages with bare concrete, and homeowners who don't yet have a go-to coating company. That's exactly the kind of search traffic a strong local SEO presence and an instant price calculator are built to capture before a competitor does.

We work with concrete coating companies across Wichita, Topeka, the Kansas City metro, and the surrounding region.

The Kansas side of the Kansas City metro — Overland Park, Olathe, Lenexa, and the surrounding suburbs — has some of the fastest new-home growth in the region, and new construction means a steady pipeline of bare garage floors that haven't been coated yet. Further west and south, Wichita and Topeka run as their own distinct markets with less competition online, which often means an easier path to ranking well for local searches.

We build campaigns that recognize this isn't one market — a company working the KC suburbs is competing against a different set of contractors, at different price points, than one working central or western Kansas.
