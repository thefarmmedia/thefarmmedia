Questions

Local SEO FAQ

How long until I see SEO results?

Most clients start seeing movement in local rankings within 60 to 90 days, with more significant gains building over 6 months as your site and profile accumulate authority.

Do I need a new website for SEO to work?

It helps, but it's not required — we can optimize an existing site. That said, a slow or poorly-structured site puts a ceiling on how far SEO alone can take you.

What is a Google Business Profile and why does it matter?

It's the listing that shows up in Google Maps and local search results with your reviews, hours, and photos. For local service businesses, it's often more important than your website ranking.

Will I show up on page one of Google?

We don't promise a specific ranking position — anyone who does is guessing or lying. What we do promise is a real, trackable improvement in visibility over time, focused on the searches that actually turn into estimates.

Do you handle Google Business Profile posts and photos too?

Yes. Keeping your profile active with regular posts and real job photos is part of what signals to Google that your business is legitimate and active — not a stale, abandoned listing.

What's the difference between SEO and paid ads?

SEO is the long game — free traffic that compounds over months. Ads are the fast lane — immediate visibility you pay for. Most clients eventually run both, but SEO is the foundation that makes ad traffic convert better too.
